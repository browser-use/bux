Best-fit partner projects

Bring a client who already has message volume and operational pain. The pilot works best when a human team is already replying manually and the operator can improve speed, consistency, and handoff quality.

Bad fit: generic chatbot demos, unsolicited broadcast bots, or workflows that need the agent to make irreversible decisions without human review.
