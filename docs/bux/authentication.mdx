BUX never sees your passwords. Logged-in sessions live in **Browser Use Profiles**, encrypted bundles of cookies and storage scoped to a single domain.

## Live login {#live-login}

The path you'll use most often.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agent hits a login wall">
    Claude Code pauses the task and asks BUX for help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BUX sends you a live URL">
    A short-lived URL like `https://live.bux.browser-use.com/<id>` arrives in Telegram and the web terminal. It expires after 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You sign in interactively">
    The URL streams the VM's Chrome to your browser via WebRTC. Type your password, do 2FA, whatever the site needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BUX captures the session">
    On success, BUX writes a profile and resumes the task from where it paused.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Importing existing logins

Skip the live login if you already have the cookies locally. With the [Browser Use Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com), pick a tab and click **Send to BUX**; the cookies for that origin upload to a new profile.

## Lifetimes and rotation

- The in-VM browser session refreshes every **240 minutes** for security. Most workflows are unaffected; expired profiles trigger a new live login.
- Rotate one profile from the dashboard: **Profiles → Rotate cookies**.
- Wipe everything: **Settings → Danger zone → Wipe profiles**.

<Note>
  Profiles are encrypted at rest with a per-account KMS key. Browser Use staff cannot decrypt them. The BUX VM's IAM role is empty, so even a compromised agent cannot reach AWS resources.
</Note>
