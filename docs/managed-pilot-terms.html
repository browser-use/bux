Good first acceptance criteria

The pilot should have a concrete pass/fail shape before work starts. A strong first workflow can usually be judged by these criteria:

  • At least one real inbound or operational thread type is handled end to end in Telegram.
  • The operator collects the required fields before escalation.
  • The human handoff is concise enough that a team member can act without rereading the full thread.
  • Escalation boundaries are visible and conservative.
  • The team can point to repeated replies, missed follow-ups, or manual context gathering that the operator reduced.
